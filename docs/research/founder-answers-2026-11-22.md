# Founder product vision (2026-11-22, in founder's words, summarized)

Paste a Kick/Twitch stream or YouTube video -> Kivlo finds the top moments and auto-edits them -> user schedules posts.
If a clip looks off, or they want a custom template, they open KivloWorkspace (built-in editor) and change anything.
Then schedule a post on TikTok, YouTube, X, or Instagram, with optional auto-generated titles and hashtags.
